About

About me.

I've spent ten years in QA inside large enterprise programmes - insurance policy systems, telecom ordering and provisioning, subscription billing and entitlements, and retail e-commerce.

The work I'm best at sits between the business and the build: reading requirements, turning them into test coverage, and being the person who says a release is ready.

I test below the screen, not just on it - the UI, API calls, direct requests in Postman and back-end logs. That helps distinguish a real defect from an environment problem.

I have ISTQB, CSM and CSPO certifications, and a Master's degree in Computer Science from The University of Texas at Dallas.
